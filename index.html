Questions

Frequently Asked

What is a local sports club?

+

Podium Athletics Club operates as a local sports club under NCAA Bylaw 13.11.2.4. This means we are open to all athletes — including high school throwers — who are legal residents within 50 miles of La Jolla, CA. Members train together in group sessions focused on the throwing events.

I'm a high school thrower. Can I join?

+

Yes! High school athletes who are legal residents within 50 miles of UC San Diego (La Jolla, CA) are welcome to join the club. This is a local sports club open to the community, not a private lesson service.

How do I know if I'm within the 50-mile radius?

+

The radius is measured from UC San Diego's campus in La Jolla, California. This generally includes most of San Diego County and southern portions of Orange County. Contact us with your home address (city) and we can verify eligibility.

What ages can join?

+

The club is open to middle school athletes, high school athletes (within 50-mile radius), masters competitors, unattached adults, and post-collegiate athletes who have completed their eligibility. All skill levels are welcome.

Do you offer discounts for talented athletes?

+

No. All club members pay the same dues regardless of ability level. This ensures fair and equal access to the club for all local athletes.
